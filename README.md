# Compass
Hackathon 9/19/26

Tool for startups / investors to know what's worth investing in and who might be interested in your business! 
Here's how it works: If you're someone looking for funding for your business, you can upload some of your videos to Compass. It'll skim through your videos and it'll scrape the Internet to find any VC Firms who may be interested in talking with you. For investors, you can upload your portfolios and find the small businesses that you think are worth betting on!
